**Role**: developer and trainer · **Period**: winter 2024–2025 · **Outcome**: bookings online, 0 double bookings since.

*A demo project.*

The hut keepers took bookings by phone and wrote them in a paper calendar kept in the valley. Two people could book the same beds without knowing it.

We chose the simplest tool that could work: a shared spreadsheet behind a form, with a daily e-mail summary. No server to maintain, and the keepers learned it in one afternoon.
