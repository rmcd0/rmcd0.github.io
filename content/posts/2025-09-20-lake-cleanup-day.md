**Role**: organiser · **Period**: spring and summer 2025 · **Outcome**: 120 volunteers, 400 kg of waste collected and sorted.

*A demo project. A project page works best when it answers three questions: what was the problem, what did you do, what changed.*

## The problem

After each storm, the shore between the harbour and the beach was covered with plastic, much of it coming from the river upstream.

## What we did

- Mapped the most affected zones with the local sailing club.
- Found partners: the municipality lent containers, a bakery fed everyone.
- Kept it simple: one morning, five teams, one shared lunch.

## What changed

The municipality now runs the day every year, and two new litter bins stand near the river mouth.
