The tools I use every day. The rule: buy second-hand, repair, keep. Only change when it really helps. (What is a /uses page? See [uses.tech](https://uses.tech/).)

## Computer

- **Lenovo ThinkPad T480s** (~2018). Finally gave it a 2nd youth in 2026 with a new battery and 24 GB of RAM. Still one of the best keyboards around, indestructible, fast: ThinkPads for the win!
- **Zorin OS**, dual-booting with a Windows I quite never use. Been Linux distro-hopping for 15 years but I settled down now. 40 is the age of maturity I guess.
- **Logitech M720 Triathlon mouse**, buttons and actions programmed with LogiOps (as Logitech's Logi Options+ isn't ported to Linux). Spent crazy amount of time getting Logiops reliably working on Linux, this project is also supposed to be published to Github soon (I know)

## Phone and network

- **Google Pixel 5**, good old phone, stock Android. Small enough for a jersey pocket, good photos, contactless payment. Mostly always been on Google phones as we can install custom ROM on it. I was on CalyxOS for years until a fatal OS crash. Still considering Graphene / Lineage / Calyx for next OS swap.
- **No fibre at home**. No TV box either. Even no internet box: small 4G modem with a 5G SIM card does the job. If you have an affordable and configurable 5G modem around, hit me up, I'm looking to upgrade
- **Quad9** as DNS resolver, on every device. VPN Unlimited or Proton VPN depending cases.

## Software

- **Browsers** are quite segmented: Opera / Firefox for daily ops, 5 Brave profiles for specific activities, Chromium for automations with Claude when needed. Firefox in virtual machines.
- **Writing**: Markdown files here, versioned with Git on [GitHub](https://github.com/rmcd0). Editors vary from Sublime for code to Zorin's native Text Editor. Classic docs in LibreOffice Suite.
- **This site**: [Eleventy](https://www.11ty.dev/) and the [Joran](https://github.com/rmcd0/joran) theme, published by GitHub Pages. More in the [colophon](/colophon/).

## Bikes

All purchased second hand. All with mechanical shifting, no battery to charge in the middle of nowhere: I'm looking for resilience, not shiny expensive stuff. Duh.

- **Specialized Sequoia**: gravel, holidays, bikepacking and everyday rides. The ultimate workhorse.
- **Cannondale CAAD12**: rim brakes, Hunt carbon 50 wheels, 11s Dura Ace groupset, raw aluminium frame. Not finished yet.
- **Cannondale CAAD8**: triple chainring Shimano 105. Used on my races and triathlons for a decade. Also comes to my bikepacking trips now.
- **Cannondale Capo**: the fixie (flip-flop). Handmade in USA back in the days, on CAAD5 frame basis. Beautiful raw aluminium + Encore 5-spoke carbon rear wheel. Gotta add some zeroes to your check if you want to buy it from me.
- **Canyon Grand Canyon CF SLX**: 29-inch hardtail for cross-country. Tan walls, inner tubes, never had a flat (touch wood). High end specs back in the days. Crazy light and probably too fast for my age now.
- **Motobécane Super Mirage** (1983): steel frame, paint job just finished. This one will have people turn back on it in the street. Stay tuned.
- Other: Crosswave city tourer sleeping here and there and that I share in my neighborood; a Kona Garbanzo Shred collector dirt bike; an indoor spinning for winter training.

I think that's pretty much it.
