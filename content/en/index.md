<p class="lead">An interpreter between tech and operations, on the shores of Lake Geneva since 2011: I talk geek to your dev team and plain business to your clients.</p>

For about fifteen years I have been running digital projects: digital transformation, information systems, document management, process redesign, and the change management that makes it all stick. I have done it in an international organisation, in pharma engineering, in electronics, and for five years in my own small company. HERMES certified, Prosci ADKAR trained, Scrum.org PSPO I.

I am now looking for my next role as **project manager, PMO or digital transformation lead**, in Geneva or Vaud, ideally where the public interest is part of the job: a local authority, a foundation, an association. The [selected projects](#h-featured) below give an idea of what I do; the [journal](/en/journal/?type=project) has the rest.

People usually describe me as curious, committed and outgoing. Pragmatic and engineering-minded, I trust facts over opinions, my own included: I am happy to be contradicted and to change my mind.

Outside office hours, I sit on the municipal council of Gland for the Greens, edit the local journal *Gland Cité*, and spend a lot of time on a bike: road, gravel, bikepacking, the longer the better. I stand for degrowth, transparency, privacy, human rights and gender equality.

This site is my corner of the web. The [journal](/en/journal/) brings home ten years of writing from Medium and my old blog: projects, opinions, tutorials and local politics, in French, English and the odd Spanish post. No cookies, no trackers, open source. The Now, Uses and Colophon pages are in French: your browser will happily translate them. Have a look around, or press <kbd>~</kbd> for a surprise.
