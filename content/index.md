🇬🇧 > Curious about everything, and especially interested in science, environment, mobility and technology.

By day, I organise other people’s information so it can be found again: information systems, analysis, processes, roll-out, training.
On the side: editor-in-chief of a small local paper, municipal councillor, engaged citizen, long-distance cyclist whenever I can, low-carbon wanderer in the Alps, tinkerer of bikes and small web tools.

I built this site to bring my work together in one place and, since I found it rather cool, I turned it into an open-source template. I named it *joran*, after the katabatic wind that rushes down from the Jura onto the lakes of Romandy, and you are free to [take it here](https://github.com/rmcd0/joran).

While we’re at it, I’m currently looking for my next job: [this way](/hire/).
