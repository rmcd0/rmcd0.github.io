🇬🇧 A Norman settled on the shores of Lake Geneva since 2011.

Information systems specialist + a busy life of associations and volunteering on the side. Municipal councillor, editor-in-chief of *Gland Cité*, long-distance cyclist whenever I can, low-carbon wanderer in the mountains and beyond, tinkerer of bikes and small web tools.

All in all, curious about everything, and especially interested in science, the environment, mobility and technology.
I like figures and analysis, writing and digressing, second-hand over new, and changing my mind when someone proves me wrong.

Gathered here: a few projects, my personal posts and public articles, my political contributions to come, and years of all sorts of writing. The site is in English, but you will find plenty of posts in French too in the Journal.

I built this site to fit my own needs and, since I found it rather cool, I turned it into an open-source template. I named it *joran*, after the katabatic wind that rushes down from the Jura onto the Swiss Plateau and the lakes of Romandy, and you are free to [grab it here](https://github.com/rmcd0/joran) and make it your own.

While we’re at it, I’m currently looking for my next job: [this way](/hire/).
