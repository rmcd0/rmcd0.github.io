How this site is made.

## Making

Static HTML generated by [Eleventy](https://www.11ty.dev/) from Markdown files, with the [Joran]({{ joran.homepage }}) theme (version {{ joran.version }}). No database, no server-side code: every page is a file, versioned on [GitHub](https://github.com/rmcd0/rmcd0.github.io) and published by GitHub Pages.

Joran is my own theme, released as open source. It is named after the *joran*, the wind that drops from the Jura onto Lake Geneva without warning, sweeps the lake and leaves again. A light website should do the same: arrive fast, leave nothing behind.

## Content

Posts written since 2015, first on my old Jekyll site (Moon theme) and on Medium, now brought home. Each post keeps its language and its old address. Images are resized and converted when the site is built.

## Typography

[Inter](https://rsms.me/inter/) for the text, [IBM Plex Mono](https://www.ibm.com/plex/) for dates and code, [VT323](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/VT323) for a secret theme. All served from this site: no call to Google or any other third party.
