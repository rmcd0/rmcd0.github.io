# Code of conduct

Joran follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, welcome newcomers, assume good intent, accept constructive criticism, and focus on what is best for the people using the theme. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks are not accepted, in any space of the project (issues, discussions, pull requests, and any other channel).

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the contact listed on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/rmcd0), or with GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) feature. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions, and temporarily or permanently ban anyone, as described in the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
